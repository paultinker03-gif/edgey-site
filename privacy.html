Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Edgey is a puzzle game. It has no ads, no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party trackers. This page explains the small amount of data it does handle.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, your best times, your sound preference and your purchase status are stored on your device only. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent, and only if you opt in

Finished levels are posted to the global leaderboard. Each time you complete one, the app sends:

  • a generated name, such as "Azure Sparrow". You never type a name; the game picks two words from a fixed list and you can shuffle it whenever you like. It says nothing about you;
  • a random identifier generated on your device, so your own entry can be updated rather than duplicated. It is never published — the leaderboard shows only a short tag derived from it, which cannot be turned back into the identifier;
  • the level you completed, your move count and your time;
  • the sequence of moves you made, which the server replays to confirm the score is genuine.

That random identifier is not derived from your device, your account, or anything about you. It is not linked to any other identifier, and it cannot be used to contact you. We do not collect your name, your email address, your contacts, your location, your photos, or your device advertising ID.

There is no free-text field anywhere in the game, so there is nothing you can type that another player could see.

Choosing not to take part

Open the player panel and turn off Post my times. Nothing further is sent, and the game remains fully playable with your times kept on your own device.

Purchases

The one-off unlock is processed by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only confirmation from the store that the purchase succeeded.

Children

Edgey is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. There is nowhere in the game to type anything, so a child cannot reveal anything about themselves through it: the leaderboard name is picked by the game from a fixed word list.

Keeping and deleting data

Leaderboard entries are kept while the board is live. To have yours removed, email the address below with the name and tag shown in your player panel, and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
