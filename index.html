How it plays

Three rules. That's the whole game.

No timers you can't beat, no lives, no energy meter. Just a shape and a set of edges that have to agree with each other. Every level tells you its par, and you are ranked on moves, not speed.

Lift

Pick up a tile and drop it on another to swap them, or on an empty slot to move it.

Turn

Tap a tile to rotate it a quarter turn. Its four marks turn with it.

Match

Every pair of touching edges must show the same mark. Match them all and the board locks shut.

24 levels

Every board a different shape

From a four-tile warm-up to a thirty-tile sprawl. Each board has its own shape, its own palette and its own kind of stuck — and every one is verified solvable before it ships.
